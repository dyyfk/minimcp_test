\section{Reading Competence from Hidden States}
\label{sec:signal}
We first study failure prediction independently of the expert path.
The representation experiments use 600 queries in five fixed pools,
with a 360-query calibration split and a 240-query test split
(\S\ref{sec:dataset}). Logistic probes read stored activations from
frozen checkpoints. Higher scores predict local-answer failure.

\subsection{Distribution transfer exposes a readout failure}
\label{sec:audit}
The final-layer probe appears useful in distribution (AUC 0.822),
but pool identity alone reaches 0.715, and the hidden state predicts
source pool at 95.8\% accuracy (Appendix~\ref{app:signal}). We
therefore emphasize leave-one-pool-out (LOPO) transfer rather than
in-distribution performance alone. With text input, the final-layer,
last-token read falls to AUC 0.372 on held-out math. Query-surface
routers also depend strongly on pool structure
(Appendix~\ref{app:router}).

Verbalized self-evaluation has different failure modes. Pre-answer
$\ptrue$ detects the held-out trap pool (AUC 0.945), where the
final-layer probe fails. Post-draft $\ptrue$ reaches 0.899 in
distribution but requires a completed draft, and can endorse
confidently wrong answers. Table~\ref{tab:signals} reports these
signals under their respective read conditions.

\subsection{Layer and position matter}
\label{sec:duplex}
A layer-by-position sweep localizes the text-input failure to late
last-token states (Figure~\ref{fig:layersweep}). Mean pooling degrades
less, and a last-token probe at layer 22 reaches \textbf{0.931} on
the same held-out math pool. A raw Qwen backbone, subsampled to match
per-pool failure rates, remains near 0.96; the Qwen2.5 control family
shows a similar ordering (Table~\ref{tab:lopo}). These comparisons
associate the degradation with the tested fine-tuned checkpoints.
They do not isolate a causal training objective.

The input condition is essential: with audio calibration and audio
input, the final-layer probe reaches 0.936 on LOPO math. We retain
L22 for its robustness to shifted text queries and modality transfer.
Mechanism tests find neither a shift in the degradation layer under
speak mode nor alignment of the inverting direction with explicit
listen/speak directions (Appendix~\ref{app:mech}). The layer sweep
establishes a useful read location, while the mechanism remains open.

\subsection{Text-to-speech representation transfer}
\label{sec:xmodal}
A probe trained on text-input hidden states and evaluated on
speech-input states reaches AUC \textbf{0.855} at L22. Transfer
stabilizes around the middle of the network, replicates on
MiniCPM-o~2.6 and Qwen2.5-Omni, and is substantially weaker in the
frozen-backbone Freeze-Omni adapter control. The mid-layer pattern
also appears on 200 SD-QA human recordings. Conversely, audio-input
verbal self-evaluation degrades on the two MiniCPM duplex generations;
adding the question transcript restores it
(Appendix~\ref{app:signal}). These are representation-transfer
results. The native escalation gate below uses a larger,
audio-derived calibration set and three positions at L22.
