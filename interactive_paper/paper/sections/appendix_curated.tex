\section{Evaluation and calibration details}
\label{app:current-protocol}

The Internal test set contains 240 queries: chat (60), factual QA (40),
knowledge (60), math (60), and SimpleQA traps (20). Each query has unit
weight, and every original query remains in the accuracy denominator. The four
external English pools retain their original membership. Archived Mandarin
diagnostics and AlpacaEval are excluded from the external macro average.

\subsection{Metric provenance}
\label{app:metric-sources}
Accuracy and escalation rates use judged native benchmark records. The expert
receives at most the final 30 seconds of the prerecorded question. TTFA uses the
complete \texttt{ttfa-v3} run (run ID \texttt{ttfa1}) on the same query cohorts
and named policies, with one attempt per query and policy. Inputs are replayed
in real time, expert requests use the audio prefix consumed at the trigger, and
both local and relay paths produce audio. The two sources are combined only at
the policy-summary level. TTFA is available for 5,904 of 5,950 attempts; 64
completed early responses contribute zero wait. Candidate and stall audio and
client playback are outside the endpoint.

\subsection{Mechanism checks}
\label{app:mech}
A speak-mode prompt template leaves final-layer LOPO math essentially unchanged
(0.362 versus 0.366), the audio--text direction is nearly orthogonal to the
failing probe, and source-pool identity remains decodable at all depths. Direct
turn-control tests are also negative: the final probe's maximum cosine with
control-token unembeddings is 0.069 versus 0.068 for a random control, and a
listen/speak prompt intervention gives cue--probe cosine 0.019 versus a 0.016
chance reference. We therefore use the depth result to choose a read location,
without assigning the late-layer decline to a particular fine-tuning loss or
training objective.

\subsection{Native feature ablation}
\label{app:native-ablation}
The matched ablation uses one native audio batch and identical training pools,
labels, splits, and logistic-head recipe. Single-state and aggregated features
have 4,096 and 12,288 dimensions. Each head fits on four pools and evaluates the
fifth; the five-pool average includes Mandarin reasoning in addition to the four
English pools. At a fixed 30\% replay budget, routing gain combines recorded
local and expert outcomes and compares with the analytical matched-random mix.

\section{Portability to a second duplex family}
\label{app:nvda}
We replay NemotronLabs-VoiceChat-11B through its native frame protocol with the
checkpoint frozen. Standardized logistic heads read L26/L30/L34 onset states;
their normalized logits are averaged. The recipe is selected using
calibration-only LOPO metrics. Its three-layer read reaches 0.818 out-of-fold
AUC and 0.816 mean external AUC. The read covers the first 640\,ms after commit,
so this experiment tests method portability rather than a second end-to-end
timing run.

\section{Gate operation and detailed results}
\label{app:gatedetail}
\label{app:livedetail}

The L22 hidden state is available after roughly 57--61\% of prefill. On one
H100, probe availability has P50/P95 of 20/25\,ms for text and 45/104\,ms for
audio. These measurements cover the local read and exclude both the network
transfer and the expert-model call.

\paragraph{Dialogue-act filtering.}
\label{app:actgate}
A second linear head uses the same L22 read to distinguish information requests
from floor-management turns. Escalation requires both the information-seeking
score $a(x)$ and failure score $s(x)$ to clear their thresholds:
\begin{equation}
d(x)=\mathbf{1}\{s(x)\geq\tau_\ell\}\,
     \mathbf{1}\{a(x)\geq\tau_a\}.
\end{equation}
On the scripted calibration stimuli, the act head has out-of-fold AUC 1.000.

\subsection{Failure type and read time}
\label{app:readtime}
A separate sweep fits the same readout family at successive native read points.
Its five-pool macro includes Mandarin reasoning. Specific-but-wrong answers
account for about 70\% of failures and are ranked at AUC 0.813; perception
errors reach 0.889, while execution slips reach 0.469. Reading two answer chunks
later raises external macro AUC from 0.755 to 0.791, quantifying the trade-off
between earlier intervention and information revealed during decoding.

\section{Judging and reproducibility}
\label{app:prompts}
\label{app:repro}
The adequacy judge receives the question, optional reference, and candidate
answer without the system identity. A response passes only when it is factually
correct, uses valid reasoning, and completely answers the question; partial,
hedged, refused, off-topic, and truncated answers fail. Representation-study
sampling and splits use seed 42. Native benchmark arms use sampled decoding and
one run per query. MiniCPM-o~4.5 runs with PyTorch 2.8.0 and Transformers 4.51.0
on single-H100 instances. The release includes calibration manifests,
out-of-fold scores, thresholds, overlap exclusions, benchmark hashes, and the
scripts needed to rebuild every reported table and figure from the recorded
evaluation data.
