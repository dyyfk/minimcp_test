\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Learned control in duplex speech models.}
Native spoken-dialogue models coordinate concurrent listening and speaking
\citep{defossez2024moshi,openbmb2026minicpmo}, using learned states,
synchronization mechanisms, or lightweight adapters
\citep{wang2024fsm,zhang2024duplex,veluri2024syncllm,wang2024freezeomni}.
BayLing-Duplex interleaves user audio, assistant text, and assistant audio; four
special tokens reduce silence, reply onset, text completion, and speech
completion to next-token prediction \citep{bayling2026duplex}. MiniCPM-o~4.5
similarly predicts a binary Listen/Speak token before content. These signals
control the floor, not whether the forthcoming answer is correct.

\paragraph{Reasoning while interacting.}
Speech models have been designed to reason before speaking, interleave
reasoning with speech, or reason while listening
\citep{chrono2026thinking}. Listen-Write-Speak uses a token schema for an
always-on visible writing channel alongside listening and speaking
\citep{zhang2026liberating}. DuplexOmni is the closest control-based handoff:
its interaction model emits \texttt{[THINK]} to invoke a pluggable background
reasoner and learns delayed-result integration from Writer--Director data
\citep{huang2026duplexomni}. MoshiRAG instead triggers retrieval from the delay
between speech onset and the first content word, then gives documents back to
the local model \citep{chien2026moshirag}. Our gate reads failure risk from the
frozen model at answer onset, before a completed local answer, and delegates the
answer to an expert. It can therefore be added to an existing duplex checkpoint
and retuned without relearning its dialogue policy.

\paragraph{Competence readout and selective routing.}
Correctness can be estimated from verbal self-evaluation
\citep{kadavath2022language,lin2022teaching}, hidden representations
\citep{azaria2023internal,burns2023discovering,marks2024geometry}, or
uncertainty across sampled answers \citep{kuhn2023semantic,farquhar2024detecting}.
Intermediate layers can retain information obscured at the output
\citep{orgad2025llms,mahaut2024factual}, and Semantic Entropy Probes amortize
sample-based uncertainty into a hidden-state read \citep{kossen2024semantic}.
Text routers allocate queries among models of different cost
\citep{chen2023frugalgpt,ding2024hybrid,ong2024routellm}, including from
prefill activations \citep{varshney2026llmrouter,lugoloobi2026failures}.
A linear probe is therefore not our novelty. Our contribution is to make such a
readout work at a native audio listen-to-speak boundary, study its survival
across speech and duplex transformations, and measure the resulting
accuracy--latency policy in a live speech pipeline with end-to-end expert
handoff.
