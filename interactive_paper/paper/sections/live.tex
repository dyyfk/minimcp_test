\subsection{Internal accuracy and timing cost}
\label{sec:live}
\label{sec:liveloop}
\label{sec:livecurve}
The upper MiniCPM block of Table~\ref{tab:transfer} reports
answer-content accuracy on the fixed 240-query internal test set:
40.8\% locally, then 46.7\%, 54.2\%, and 62.9\% for conservative,
balanced, and aggressive. Their realized escalation rates are
8.3\%, 25.0\%, and 51.7\% (Appendix~\ref{app:transferdetail}).
Aggressive exceeds the table's random reference of 56.1\% by
6.8 percentage points. Per-tier internal comparisons are in
Table~\ref{tab:live}. Aggressive recovers 74.6\% of the measured
local-to-always accuracy gap while using 51.7\% expert calls.
This measures selection value relative to the recorded endpoints.

Gains vary across the fixed internal strata: factual QA improves by
52.5 points, while math accuracy is unchanged. Of 60 knowledge
questions, 43 exceed the expert's input window. These descriptive
results identify an input constraint and task differences, without
attributing the accuracy gap to truncation or changing the test
mixture (Appendix~\ref{app:internal-strata}).

The independent \texttt{ttfa-v3} run measures server time to first
answer audio (Figure~\ref{fig:dualview}). Internal P50 is 0.70\,s
locally, 1.61\,s at balanced, and 2.87\,s at aggressive. Aggressive
has mean 4.59\,s and P95 18.14\,s; always has mean 8.01\,s and
P50 6.67\,s. Local signed mean is $-0.61$\,s: 17/240 attempts
(7.1\%) respond before input end. These early responses are retained
without assuming better interaction or correctness. Table~\ref{tab:latency}
counts failures and reports timing among completed sessions. These
answers remain unjudged: 62.9\% accuracy and 4.59\,s mean TTFA are
separate results, not a demonstrated joint operating point.
