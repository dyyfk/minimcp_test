\subsection{Layer and position matter}
\label{sec:duplex}
We first evaluate the read location independently of the expert path.
A layer-by-position sweep under leave-one-pool-out (LOPO) transfer
localizes the text-input failure to late last-token states
(Figure~\ref{fig:layersweep}). A last-token probe at L22 reaches
\textbf{0.931} AUC on held-out math, compared with the final-layer
baseline of 0.372, while mean pooling reduces the
late-layer degradation. A raw Qwen backbone, subsampled to match
per-pool failure rates, remains near 0.96; the Qwen2.5 control family
shows a similar ordering (Table~\ref{tab:lopo}). These comparisons
associate the degradation with the tested fine-tuned checkpoints,
without isolating a causal training objective.

  LOPO math AUC across layers: late last-token reads deteriorate on

With audio calibration and audio input, the final-layer probe reaches
0.936 on LOPO math. We retain L22 for robustness across the tested
conditions; the mechanism behind the text-input degradation remains
open (Appendix~\ref{app:mech}).

The text-input cliff does not carry over to the deployed read point
unchanged. A layer $\times$ readout ablation at the native audio
commit moment, with all four cells recomputed on one
official-configuration batch under identical training pools, labels,
splits, and head recipe, separates depth from position aggregation
(Figure~\ref{fig:native-ablation}). With a single last-token read the
mid layer has no advantage (0.595 at L22 vs.\ 0.654 at the final
layer, external-pool LOPO); the three-position aggregation recovers
the signal ($+$0.10 AUC at L22, $+$0.01 at L35), and the mid-layer
edge exists only for the aggregated read (0.695 vs.\ 0.662). At a
matched 30\% escalation budget every cell except single-token L22
clears random routing by $+$2.8 to $+$3.5 accuracy points,
differences within the replication floor. The mid-layer advantage is
therefore specific to the text input condition; at the native commit
point the position aggregation carries the read, with a residual
mid-depth ranking edge.

\subsection{Text-to-speech representation transfer}
\label{sec:xmodal}
A text-calibrated L22 probe evaluated on speech-input states reaches
\textbf{0.855} AUC. Transfer stabilizes around the middle of the
network, replicates on MiniCPM-o~2.6 and Qwen2.5-Omni, and is weaker
in the frozen-backbone Freeze-Omni adapter control. The mid-layer
pattern also appears on 200 SD-QA human recordings
(Appendix~\ref{app:signal}). These results concern representation
transfer. The escalation experiments below use a separately fitted
native probe with three L22 positions, as defined in
\S\ref{sec:readlocation}.
